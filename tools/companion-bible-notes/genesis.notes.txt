# ---------------------------------------------------------------------
# Sources and how they were combined
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------
# Unlike Philemon, Genesis was read from the page scans themselves. The
# "_text.pdf" carries the page images (JBIG2/JPX); each page's note column
# and bottom-of-page block were rendered at 200 dpi and transcribed from
# the image, with the HOCR word boxes used to locate the note column and
# as a cross-check. The OCR alone is not usable here: it shatters the
# full-width bottom notes and garbles roughly one line in ten.
#
# After transcription, two automated passes:
#   - every note-column line of the HOCR was matched against this file to
#     catch anything skipped (none was; the hits were KJV text set in the
#     right-hand column on some pages);
#   - every word of this file was looked up in the HOCR; the leftovers are
#     Hebrew transliterations, outline labels, and words the OCR mangled,
#     each re-checked against the scan.
#
# A verse whose notes run over a page break is joined into one entry.
#
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------
# Correction policy
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------
# Hebrew transliteration is written in plain ASCII (no macrons or
# breathings), as elsewhere in this app: n'shamah, 'adham, tohu va bohu.

49:31 | Abraham. The five previously buried there form an acrostic of the sixth. Isaac, Sarah, Rebekah, Abraham, Leah = Israel (Heb. 11. 13).
49:33 | yielded up the ghost. The spirit going to God who gave it (Ecc. 12. 7. Num. 16. 22; 27. 16). gathered unto his people. Refers to body, as the previous gathering (v. 33) refers to his feet. See on v. 29. He was aged 147.
50:1 | wept. See note on 42. 24.
50:2 | Israel. See notes on 32. 28; 43. 8; 45. 26, 28.
50:3 | for so, &c. Israelites never embalmed. Hence the need of this parenthetical explanation. See Ap. 6.
50:4 | Joseph spake. He could not speak before, for, in mourning, Egyptians never shaved, and Joseph, therefore, was not presentable. See on 41. 14.
50:5 | Lo. Fig. Asterismos. Ap. 6. digged. Hence, probably rock-hewn.
50:7 | 7-10 And. Note the Fig. Polysyndeton (Ap. 6), emphasising each party.
50:11 | Atad. Probably named after the owner, as 2 Sam. 6. 6, "Nachon's"; 2 Sam. 24. 16, "Araunah's". Abel-mizraim = the mourning of the Egyptians.
50:13 | his sons ... buried him. See note on Acts 7. 16. bought. Cp. 23. 16.
50:15 | The final section of Genesis.
50:17 | God. Heb. Elohim. Ap. 4. Joseph wept. See note on 42. 24.
50:18 | fell down. Cp. 37. 7-10. Behold. Fig. Asterismos. Ap. 6.
50:19 | am I, &c. Fig. Erotesis (Ap. 6). Sept. reads "I am in the place of God". The Syr. and Arab. read "I fear God".
50:20 | God meant it, &c. Cp. Ps. 105. 17. Heb. Elohim, Ap. 4. alive. See on 45. 5.
50:21 | kindly. Heb. spake unto their hearts.
50:23 | Machir. Cp. Num. 26. 29; 32. 39. Josh. 17. 1. brought up, &c. Heb. born. See 30. 3: i.e., were adopted by him as soon as they were born.
50:24 | surely visit you. (Ex. 3. 16.) Fig. Polyptoton. Ap. 6. Heb. "visiting will visit you"; used for great emphasis. See note on 26. 28. This was the faith of Joseph referred to in Heb. 11. 22. He had "heard" (Rom. 10. 17) and believed what God had said to Abraham. Gen. 12. 7; 15. 18; 13. 14, 15; Isaac. 26. 3, 4; Jacob. 28. 13; 35. 12; 48. 1-4. All three names are united and discriminated in Ex. 2. 24; 3. 6. Ps. 105. 9, 10. And cp. Ex. 6. 3, 4. Deut. 11. 21. Luke 1. 72, 73, &c.
50:25 | carry up my bones from hence. Which they did. See Ex. 13. 19. Josh. 24. 32. So Jacob had charged them, Gen. 49. 29, 30, and so they had done, Gen. 50. 7-13. hence. Some codices, with Sam., Sept., Syr., and one printed edition (1494), add "with you".
50:26 | a coffin. Thus the book of Genesis begins with God, and ends with man. It begins with the creation of the heavens above, and ends with "a coffin in Egypt".
