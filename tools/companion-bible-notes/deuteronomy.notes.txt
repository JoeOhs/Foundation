0 | TITLE. Moses. See Ap. 2 and 47. Called: by the Sept. translators, and from them by Jerome, in the Vulgate. Deuteronomy = Second Law, from 17. 18, where "copy of the law" is so rendered from Rabbinic mishneh, a doubling. Hebrew Title = Hadd'barim = "the words". It is the fifth book, concluding the five books of the Pentateuch (see Ap. 10). No scroll of the Pentateuch ever seen with more than these five. See Ap. 46 and 47. The whole history of the book took place in the eleventh month of the fortieth year. Only the last fifteen chapters of Numbers refer to the time of Deut.
