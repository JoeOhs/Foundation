A1 |  |
  B1 |  | HOSEA. Like AMOS (B2), dated in the reigns of Kings of Judah, and in that of Jeroboam II, the King of Israel. HOSEA probably belonged (like Amos) to the Ten Tribes (perhaps to Reuben); and prophesied both to the house of Israel, and to the house of Judah apart.
    C1 |  | JOEL. Undated. General: concerning (1) the Gentile kingdoms, and (2) "the Day of the Lord."
  B2 |  | AMOS. Like HOSEA (B1), dated in the reigns of one King of Judah, and in that of Jeroboam II, one King of Israel. AMOS probably belonged (like Hosea) to the Ten Tribes (perhaps to Asher); and prophesied to the house of Israel and to the house of Judah apart.
